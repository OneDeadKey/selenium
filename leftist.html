Spaceback Variant

If you prefer to have Space under the left thumb, this option might suit your needs.

Every sentence and every proper noun start with an uppercase letter and follow a space; so, to avoid a same-thumb bigram, Space and Shift must be under two different thumbs; and as the positions of Shift and Sym are set by Arsenik, we need a Space key under the right thumb.

The right Space key cannot be moved without breaking the compatibility with Arsenik, but it can be duplicated on the left side:

  • Backspace replaces Enter under the right thumb;
  • Enter remains accessible from the Sym layer;
  • pro: Space can be used while the right hand holds the mouse;
  • con: the left nav now has tap-preferred priority, and left-hand shortcuts now require a long press to work.

This variant is still experimental. Testing and feedback are welcome.
